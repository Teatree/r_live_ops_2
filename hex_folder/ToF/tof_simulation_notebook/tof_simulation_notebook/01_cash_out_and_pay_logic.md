Logic for cash out:
- Player has spent more than the "Ready to lose" amount of coins OR has reached "cash-out" amount of coin value of rewards with the price of next pig being more than 50% of rewards so far.
- AND the next stage has pigs

Logic to pay the pig:
- Player will not end up spending more than the "Ready to lose" amount of coins OR the cost of the pig is less than 80% of the rewards collected so far.
